Firmware Intallation

To install the firmware:

Turn your printer and go to settings > WLAN / Network and toggle on LAN Mode Only.

DO NOT SKIP THE STEP ABOVE.

Connect Bamby to a usb, after Bamby is connected to the printer and only then you can try to turn "LAN Mode Only" off.

To make sure Bamby will connect to the printer, the 2 steps above must be done every time.

Make sure you're on chrome or edge browser.

After pressing the "Install the firmware" button:

  • Connect the esp32 to the computer with usb.
  • First you will need to choose the correct serial port, if you don't know which one, with the serial port window open disconnect the usb, after reconnecting the usb it will show the connected port, choose it and press connect.
  • Press Install ESP32 Bamby.
  • Wait for the firmware to finish the installation.

After firmware installation

The time frame for this section is 10 minutes. If you haven't enter all the data and press the button within 10 minutes, the device will reboot.

everything is fine, just start this section again from the beginning, no need to install the firmware again.

  • Go to settings and note the Credentials: Your printer will display an IP Address and a Verification/Access Code, you will need them later.
  • On the Printer Screen: Navigate to Settings > General > Device Info look for serial number, you will need it later.
  • Go to the WIFI settings on your phone or computer, Available networks > click on ESP BAMBY enter password:123456789 and connect to it.
  • Before the next step, make sure it remains connected to ESP Bamby and that the phone or computer does not reconnect to the Internet.
  • Open the browser and go to: 192.168.4.18
  • In the form that opened press Scan for Networks choose your wifi ssid, enter your wifi password.
  • In the Bambu Printer Setup section: enter your Printer ip Address, Printer Access Code, Printer Serial Number (collected earlier).
  • Press Save & Connect.
  • Wait 15 seconds and disconnect the usb.
  • Reconnect the usb, wait a few seconds for it to connect to the printer.
  • Enjoy Bamby.
