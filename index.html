ESP32 Bamby

Meet Bamby

Bamby the wireless bambu printers monitor.

Bamby will show live data from the printer with cute expressions.

It needs only 2 components: esp32-c3 super mini and 1.28" round tft screen gc9a01.

The case can be 3d printed.

Video at the buttom of the page.

Components

Just 2 components: ESP32-C3 and GC9A01.

Why do we need it?

We need monitoring davice for for our printer, Just like monitoring davice for a baby.

Bamby will show live data:

  • Heatbed Temperature.
  • Nozzle Temperature.
  • File Name.
  • Number Of Layers.
  • Time Left.
  • Visual prograss bar with precentage.
