ESP32 Bamby

Meet Bamby

Bamby the wireless bambu printers monitor.

Bamby will show live data from the printer with cute expressions.

It needs only 2 components: esp32-c3 super mini and 1.28" round tft screen gc9a01.

The case can be 3d printed.
